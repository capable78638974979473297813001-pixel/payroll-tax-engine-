Oregon Paid Leave, statewide transit tax and WBF

Three employee-side lines that appear with Oregon income tax.

An Oregon paycheck has more state lines than the income tax. The engine returns Paid Leave Oregon, the statewide transit tax and the Workers' Benefit Fund assessment on a $3,000.00 biweekly check, and the employer matches the assessment.

What the engine says about each

  • Oregon Paid Leave (Employee) ($18.00): $3000.00 @ 0.60%, capped at $184500.00/yr ($0.00 YTD already counted)
  • Oregon Statewide Transit Tax ($3.00): $3000.00 @ 0.100%, no wage cap
  • Oregon Workers' Benefit Fund (Employee) ($0.72): 80 hours (flat-rate method, hoursWorked not supplied) × $0.0090/hr

Caps and hours

Paid Leave Oregon is capped at a yearly wage limit, tracked in ytd.statePaidLeave.OR. The Workers' Benefit Fund assessment is charged per hour worked, split equally between employee and employer. When the request does not carry hoursWorked the engine assumes a flat-rate period, which its detail text states. Send the hours for an exact figure.

Portland-area taxes

Employees in the Portland region may also have the Metro Supportive Housing Services tax and the Multnomah County Preschool for All tax withheld; both are threshold-triggered taxes on higher incomes. Employers in particular transit districts owe payroll taxes of their own. The engine's Oregon rule file carries them; see coverage for the locations.

Questions

What does Oregon withhold besides income tax?

Paid Leave Oregon contributions, the statewide transit tax, and the employee half of the Workers' Benefit Fund assessment, each as its own line.

How is the Workers' Benefit Fund assessment charged?

Per hour worked, split equally between employee and employer. Send hoursWorked for an exact figure; otherwise the engine uses a flat-rate period and says so.
