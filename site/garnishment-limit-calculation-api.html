Garnishment limit calculation API

How much of a paycheck can a garnishment take? The answer depends on the order and the state.

When a creditor, agency or court orders part of an employee's pay withheld, the amount that may be taken is capped by law. The cap depends on the kind of order and, for ordinary consumer debt, on state law, which can be stricter than the federal limit.

One call

Add garnishments to a paycheck request and the response includes a garnishment result next to the tax lines. The request needs workState.code, because state law decides the limit on an ordinary consumer garnishment, and each order needs your own identifier so the result can be matched back to it.

"garnishments": [ { "id": "order-1", ...the order... } ]

It is calculated on the same engine as the rest of the paycheck and billed as one call; the garnishment is a post-tax deduction, so it comes out after the taxes.

What it is not

It applies the legal limit to the orders you send. It does not interpret a legal order or send the money to the creditor, and a payroll specialist or counsel should confirm an order's terms.

Questions

How much of a paycheck can be garnished?

It depends on the type of order and the state. For ordinary consumer debt, federal law sets a cap and state law can set a lower one, so the work state matters.

Is the garnishment taken before or after tax?

After. A garnishment is a post-tax deduction, so the taxes are calculated first and the garnishment comes out of the pay that is left.
