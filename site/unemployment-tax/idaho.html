Unemployment tax by state

Idaho unemployment tax: wage base and rate

What an employer pays in Idaho, from the same rule files the API uses.

Idaho's state unemployment tax (SUTA, also called SUI) is paid by the employer, on each employee's wages up to the state's wage base, which is $58,300 in 2026. Once an employee's wages for the year pass the base, no more state unemployment tax is due on them.

Idaho unemployment tax at a glance

2027 state wage base$58,300
New-employer rate (2026)1%

Use your own rate

The new-employer rate is a starting point. After your first period the state assigns a rate from your experience, and it is on your rate notice. Send it and the engine uses it instead:

"employer": { "stateUnemploymentRate": { "ID": 0.031 } }

Questions

What is the unemployment tax wage base in Idaho?

$58,300 for 2026 in Omnia.tax's rule files. Idaho's state unemployment tax applies only to each employee's wages up to that amount in a year.

What unemployment tax rate does a new employer pay in Idaho?

1% in Omnia.tax's 2026 rules, unless you send your own assigned rate in employer.stateUnemploymentRate. An established employer's rate is assigned by the state, so check your rate notice.

How much is the maximum state unemployment tax per employee in Idaho?

At the new-employer rate of 1%, up to $583.00 per employee per year ($58,300 × 1%). Your own rate changes that figure.
