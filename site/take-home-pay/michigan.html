Take-home pay in Michigan

What nine salaries take home in Michigan, calculated by the same engine the API uses.

Michigan taxes wages at a flat 4.25%. The table is the engine's own result for a single filer paid biweekly with no pre-tax deductions, no allowances and no local tax, check dated 2026-09-15. Federal income tax is the employee's W-4 result; "State" is Michigan income tax and any state-level employee contribution such as paid leave or disability.

How it compares

On $75,000, employee taxes in Michigan are 22.1% of gross pay, and 25.1% on $100,000. Of the $75,000 case, 4.3% is Michigan tax and 10.2% is federal income tax.

For employers

These are the employee's side. The employer pays more on top: see Michigan payroll tax and Michigan unemployment tax. To get these numbers from your own software, use the paycheck calculator API.

Questions

How much is take-home pay on a $75,000 salary in Michigan?

About $2,246.34 per biweekly paycheck, or $58,404.84 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions, using Omnia.tax's 2026 rules. That is $638.28 of tax per paycheck: $295.00 federal income tax, $220.68 Social Security and Medicare, and $122.60 Michigan tax.

How much is take-home pay on $100,000 in Michigan?

About $2,881.92 per biweekly paycheck, or $74,929.92 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions. Employee taxes are 25.1% of gross.

Does Michigan have a state income tax on wages?

Yes, a flat 4.25% in Omnia.tax's 2026 rules, applied after the state's allowances and deductions.
