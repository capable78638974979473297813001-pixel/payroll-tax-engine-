Take-home pay in Louisiana

What nine salaries take home in Louisiana, calculated by the same engine the API uses.

Louisiana withholds from its own tables or formula, so the state column rises with pay. The table is the engine's own result for a single filer paid biweekly with no pre-tax deductions, no allowances and no local tax, check dated 2026-09-15. Federal income tax is the employee's W-4 result; "State" is Louisiana income tax and any state-level employee contribution such as paid leave or disability.

How it compares

On $75,000, employee taxes in Louisiana are 21.0% of gross pay, and 23.9% on $100,000. Of the $75,000 case, 3.1% is Louisiana tax and 10.2% is federal income tax.

For employers

These are the employee's side. The employer pays more on top: see Louisiana payroll tax and Louisiana unemployment tax. To get these numbers from your own software, use the paycheck calculator API.

Questions

How much is take-home pay on a $75,000 salary in Louisiana?

About $2,279.81 per biweekly paycheck, or $59,275.06 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions, using Omnia.tax's 2026 rules. That is $604.81 of tax per paycheck: $295.00 federal income tax, $220.68 Social Security and Medicare, and $89.13 Louisiana tax.

How much is take-home pay on $100,000 in Louisiana?

About $2,926.53 per biweekly paycheck, or $76,089.78 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions. Employee taxes are 23.9% of gross.

Does Louisiana have a state income tax on wages?

Yes. Louisiana withholds income tax from its own published tables or formula.
