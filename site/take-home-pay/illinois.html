Take-home pay in Illinois

What nine salaries take home in Illinois, calculated by the same engine the API uses.

Illinois taxes wages at a flat 4.95%. The table is the engine's own result for a single filer paid biweekly with no pre-tax deductions, no allowances and no local tax, check dated 2026-09-15. Federal income tax is the employee's W-4 result; "State" is Illinois income tax and any state-level employee contribution such as paid leave or disability.

How it compares

On $75,000, employee taxes in Illinois are 22.8% of gross pay, and 25.8% on $100,000. Of the $75,000 case, 5.0% is Illinois tax and 10.2% is federal income tax.

For employers

These are the employee's side. The employer pays more on top: see Illinois payroll tax and Illinois unemployment tax. To get these numbers from your own software, use the paycheck calculator API.

Questions

How much is take-home pay on a $75,000 salary in Illinois?

About $2,226.15 per biweekly paycheck, or $57,879.90 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions, using Omnia.tax's 2026 rules. That is $658.47 of tax per paycheck: $295.00 federal income tax, $220.68 Social Security and Medicare, and $142.79 Illinois tax.

How much is take-home pay on $100,000 in Illinois?

About $2,855.00 per biweekly paycheck, or $74,230.00 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions. Employee taxes are 25.8% of gross.

Does Illinois have a state income tax on wages?

Yes, a flat 4.95% in Omnia.tax's 2026 rules, applied after the state's allowances and deductions.
