Take-home pay in North Dakota

What nine salaries take home in North Dakota, calculated by the same engine the API uses.

North Dakota withholds from its own tables or formula, so the state column rises with pay. The table is the engine's own result for a single filer paid biweekly with no pre-tax deductions, no allowances and no local tax, check dated 2026-09-15. Federal income tax is the employee's W-4 result; "State" is North Dakota income tax and any state-level employee contribution such as paid leave or disability.

How it compares

On $75,000, employee taxes in North Dakota are 18.3% of gross pay, and 21.7% on $100,000. Of the $75,000 case, 0.5% is North Dakota tax and 10.2% is federal income tax.

For employers

These are the employee's side. The employer pays more on top: see North Dakota payroll tax and North Dakota unemployment tax. To get these numbers from your own software, use the paycheck calculator API.

Questions

How much is take-home pay on a $75,000 salary in North Dakota?

About $2,355.94 per biweekly paycheck, or $61,254.44 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions, using Omnia.tax's 2026 rules. That is $528.68 of tax per paycheck: $295.00 federal income tax, $220.68 Social Security and Medicare, and $13.00 North Dakota tax.

How much is take-home pay on $100,000 in North Dakota?

About $3,013.38 per biweekly paycheck, or $78,347.88 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions. Employee taxes are 21.7% of gross.

Does North Dakota have a state income tax on wages?

Yes. North Dakota withholds income tax from its own published tables or formula.
