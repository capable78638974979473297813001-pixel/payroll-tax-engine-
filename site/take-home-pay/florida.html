Take-home pay in Florida

What nine salaries take home in Florida, calculated by the same engine the API uses.

Florida does not tax wages, so the only taxes on the paycheck are federal income tax and FICA. The table is the engine's own result for a single filer paid biweekly with no pre-tax deductions, no allowances and no local tax, check dated 2026-09-15. Federal income tax is the employee's W-4 result; "State" is Florida income tax and any state-level employee contribution such as paid leave or disability.

How it compares

On $75,000, employee taxes in Florida are 17.9% of gross pay, and 20.8% on $100,000. Of the $75,000 case, 0.0% is Florida tax and 10.2% is federal income tax.

For employers

These are the employee's side. The employer pays more on top: see Florida payroll tax and Florida unemployment tax. To get these numbers from your own software, use the paycheck calculator API.

Questions

How much is take-home pay on a $75,000 salary in Florida?

About $2,368.94 per biweekly paycheck, or $61,592.44 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions, using Omnia.tax's 2026 rules. That is $515.68 of tax per paycheck: $295.00 federal income tax, $220.68 Social Security and Medicare, and $0.00 Florida tax.

How much is take-home pay on $100,000 in Florida?

About $3,045.38 per biweekly paycheck, or $79,179.88 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions. Employee taxes are 20.8% of gross.

Does Florida have a state income tax on wages?

No. Florida does not tax wages in Omnia.tax's 2026 rules.
