Take-home pay in Arizona

What nine salaries take home in Arizona, calculated by the same engine the API uses.

Arizona withholds from its own tables or formula, so the state column rises with pay. The table is the engine's own result for a single filer paid biweekly with no pre-tax deductions, no allowances and no local tax, check dated 2026-09-15. Federal income tax is the employee's W-4 result; "State" is Arizona income tax and any state-level employee contribution such as paid leave or disability.

How it compares

On $75,000, employee taxes in Arizona are 19.9% of gross pay, and 22.8% on $100,000. Of the $75,000 case, 2.0% is Arizona tax and 10.2% is federal income tax.

For employers

These are the employee's side. The employer pays more on top: see Arizona payroll tax and Arizona unemployment tax. To get these numbers from your own software, use the paycheck calculator API.

Questions

How much is take-home pay on a $75,000 salary in Arizona?

About $2,311.25 per biweekly paycheck, or $60,092.50 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions, using Omnia.tax's 2026 rules. That is $573.37 of tax per paycheck: $295.00 federal income tax, $220.68 Social Security and Medicare, and $57.69 Arizona tax.

How much is take-home pay on $100,000 in Arizona?

About $2,968.46 per biweekly paycheck, or $77,179.96 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions. Employee taxes are 22.8% of gross.

Does Arizona have a state income tax on wages?

Yes. Arizona withholds income tax from its own published tables or formula.
