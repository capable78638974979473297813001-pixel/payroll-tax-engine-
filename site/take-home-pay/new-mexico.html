Take-home pay in New Mexico

What nine salaries take home in New Mexico, calculated by the same engine the API uses.

New Mexico withholds from its own tables or formula, so the state column rises with pay. The table is the engine's own result for a single filer paid biweekly with no pre-tax deductions, no allowances and no local tax, check dated 2026-09-15. Federal income tax is the employee's W-4 result; "State" is New Mexico income tax and any state-level employee contribution such as paid leave or disability.

How it compares

On $75,000, employee taxes in New Mexico are 21.5% of gross pay, and 24.8% on $100,000. Of the $75,000 case, 3.7% is New Mexico tax and 10.2% is federal income tax.

For employers

These are the employee's side. The employer pays more on top: see New Mexico payroll tax and New Mexico unemployment tax. To get these numbers from your own software, use the paycheck calculator API.

Questions

How much is take-home pay on a $75,000 salary in New Mexico?

About $2,263.25 per biweekly paycheck, or $58,844.50 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions, using Omnia.tax's 2026 rules. That is $621.37 of tax per paycheck: $295.00 federal income tax, $220.68 Social Security and Medicare, and $105.69 New Mexico tax.

How much is take-home pay on $100,000 in New Mexico?

About $2,892.57 per biweekly paycheck, or $75,206.82 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions. Employee taxes are 24.8% of gross.

Does New Mexico have a state income tax on wages?

Yes. New Mexico withholds income tax from its own published tables or formula.
