Take-home pay in Alaska

What nine salaries take home in Alaska, calculated by the same engine the API uses.

Alaska does not tax wages, so the only taxes on the paycheck are federal income tax and FICA. The table is the engine's own result for a single filer paid biweekly with no pre-tax deductions, no allowances and no local tax, check dated 2026-09-15. Federal income tax is the employee's W-4 result; "State" is Alaska income tax and any state-level employee contribution such as paid leave or disability.

How it compares

On $75,000, employee taxes in Alaska are 18.4% of gross pay, and 21.3% on $100,000. Of the $75,000 case, 0.5% is Alaska tax and 10.2% is federal income tax.

For employers

These are the employee's side. The employer pays more on top: see Alaska payroll tax and Alaska unemployment tax. To get these numbers from your own software, use the paycheck calculator API.

Questions

How much is take-home pay on a $75,000 salary in Alaska?

About $2,354.52 per biweekly paycheck, or $61,217.52 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions, using Omnia.tax's 2026 rules. That is $530.10 of tax per paycheck: $295.00 federal income tax, $220.68 Social Security and Medicare, and $14.42 Alaska tax.

How much is take-home pay on $100,000 in Alaska?

About $3,026.15 per biweekly paycheck, or $78,679.90 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions. Employee taxes are 21.3% of gross.

Does Alaska have a state income tax on wages?

No. Alaska does not tax wages in Omnia.tax's 2026 rules.
