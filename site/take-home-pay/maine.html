Take-home pay in Maine

What nine salaries take home in Maine, calculated by the same engine the API uses.

Maine withholds from its own tables or formula, so the state column rises with pay. The table is the engine's own result for a single filer paid biweekly with no pre-tax deductions, no allowances and no local tax, check dated 2026-09-15. Federal income tax is the employee's W-4 result; "State" is Maine income tax and any state-level employee contribution such as paid leave or disability.

How it compares

On $75,000, employee taxes in Maine are 23.6% of gross pay, and 27.0% on $100,000. Of the $75,000 case, 5.7% is Maine tax and 10.2% is federal income tax.

For employers

These are the employee's side. The employer pays more on top: see Maine payroll tax and Maine unemployment tax. To get these numbers from your own software, use the paycheck calculator API.

Questions

How much is take-home pay on a $75,000 salary in Maine?

About $2,203.52 per biweekly paycheck, or $57,291.52 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions, using Omnia.tax's 2026 rules. That is $681.10 of tax per paycheck: $295.00 federal income tax, $220.68 Social Security and Medicare, and $165.42 Maine tax.

How much is take-home pay on $100,000 in Maine?

About $2,806.15 per biweekly paycheck, or $72,959.90 over 26 paychecks, for a single filer with no deductions. Employee taxes are 27.0% of gross.

Does Maine have a state income tax on wages?

Yes. Maine withholds income tax from its own published tables or formula.
