Payroll tax API response fields

What each field in the paycheck response means.

A paycheck response is small, and every field earns its place. Here is what each one means, with values from a real run: an employee in Columbus, Ohio paid $3,000.00 biweekly.

Totals

FieldValue in the example
grossPay300000 ($3,000.00)
pretaxDeductions0
posttaxDeductions0
employeeTaxTotal70065 ($700.65)
employerTaxTotal33300 ($333.00), not taken from the employee
netPay229935 ($2,299.35)

The identity to test: netPay = grossPay − pretaxDeductions − employeeTaxTotal − posttaxDeductions. Here $3,000.00 − $0.00 − $700.65 − $0.00 = $2,299.35.

A tax line

FieldMeaning
idA stable identifier, such as US_FIT or OH_LOCAL.
nameA name suitable for a pay stub.
payeremployee (withheld from pay) or employer (a cost on top).
jurisdictionFederal, state or local.
taxableWagesThe wages this tax was applied to, which can differ from gross.
amountThe tax, in cents.
detailOne sentence on how the amount was reached.

Optional fields

  • notices: lines resting on a weaker source than the levying body's own form, each with the tax id, a tier and a note.
  • warnings: inputs that need a second look, and provisional-year notices.
  • electiveDeferrals: what stayed pre-tax after the annual retirement limits.

Questions

What is the difference between taxableWages and grossPay?

grossPay is total earnings. taxableWages is the amount a particular tax was applied to, after deductions that are pre-tax for that tax and after any cap, so it can differ from line to line.

Why is employerTaxTotal not part of net pay?

Employer taxes are a cost the employer pays on top of gross pay. They are never deducted from the employee, so net pay ignores them.
