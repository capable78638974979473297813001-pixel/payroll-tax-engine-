Local taxes

Kansas City and St. Louis earnings tax

Missouri has exactly two cities with this power.

Missouri allows only two cities, Kansas City and St. Louis, to levy an earnings tax on wages. Both apply to residents wherever they work, and to nonresidents on wages earned in the city. The engine switches them on from the certificate's locality.

Two checks

City earnings taxEmployer city taxNet pay
Kansas City$30.00$0.00$2,315.12
St. Louis$30.00$15.00$2,315.12

Both earnings taxes are 1% of wages. St. Louis also charges employers a payroll expense tax on top, $15.00 on this check. The engine says: $3000.00 @ 0.50%, employer-only, layered on top of the employee earnings tax

In the request

"workState": { "code": "MO", "certificate": { "locality": "Kansas City" } }

The locality is the caller's determination of residence or work location in one of the two cities. The engine does not guess.

Notes

  • Missouri state withholding is a separate line.
  • The employer payroll expense tax does not reduce net pay.
  • These are city taxes administered by the cities, not by the state.

Questions

Which Missouri cities have an earnings tax?

Kansas City and St. Louis. They are the only two cities in Missouri with the power to levy one.

Does the employer pay anything extra in St. Louis?

Yes. St. Louis has a payroll expense tax charged to employers on top of the employee earnings tax.
