Who it is for

Payroll tax calculator for small business owners

What you pay on top of wages, and what the employee takes home.

If you run a small business and are about to pay someone, two numbers matter: what they take home and what it costs you. Both depend on where the work is done. This calculator gives both for one paycheck, with the lines named, so you can plan a hire or sanity-check your payroll provider.

Before the first hire

  • Register as an employer with the IRS and the state unemployment agency. The calculator does not do this.
  • Get a Form W-4 and any state withholding certificate from the employee.
  • Know where the work is done: it decides state and local tax.
  • Decide how you will deposit tax and file returns, or choose a provider that does.

What the calculator will not do

It calculates. It does not register your business, run payroll, file or deposit. Small employers usually use a payroll service for those and a calculator to check it or to plan. See taxes on your first employee.

Questions

How much does an employee cost on top of wages?

The employer pays about 7.65% for Social Security and Medicare up to the Social Security wage base, plus federal and state unemployment tax early in the year and any state paid-leave contributions. The calculator shows the exact lines.

Do I need an API?

No. The browser calculator is enough for planning and checking. The API is for software that calculates paychecks automatically.
