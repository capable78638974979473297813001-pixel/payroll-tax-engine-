Social Security wage base: $184,500 for 2026

The annual limit on wages subject to Social Security tax, and what follows from it.

Social Security tax applies only to wages up to the annual wage base. The base is $184,500 for 2026 and $192,300 for 2027.

When the tax stops

An employee earning $150,000 a year never reaches it. One earning $300,000 reaches the base after 62% of the year's pay, and from then on that paycheck carries no Social Security tax, only Medicare. The paycheck that crosses the base is partly taxed; see the worked example.

Why a calculator needs year-to-date wages

Whether a paycheck is taxed depends on what was paid before it. Without year-to-date Social Security wages in the request, an engine cannot know whether the cap has been reached, so ytd.socialSecurity is an input to the API, not an optional extra.

Questions

What is the Social Security wage base for 2026?

$184,500. Wages above it are not subject to Social Security tax, though Medicare still applies. For 2027 it is $192,300.

What is the most Social Security tax an employee pays in 2026?

$184,500 times 6.2%, which is $11,439.00. The employer pays the same amount again.
