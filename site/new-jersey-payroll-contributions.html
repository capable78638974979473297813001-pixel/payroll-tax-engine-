New Jersey employee payroll contributions

Four employee-side lines beside New Jersey income tax.

New Jersey is unusual for the number of contributions it takes from the employee as well as the employer. Besides income tax, a $3,000.00 biweekly check shows an employee unemployment and workforce contribution, a temporary disability contribution and a family leave insurance contribution, and the employer pays a disability contribution of its own.

Employee lines: income tax $121.05, unemployment and workforce $12.75, temporary disability $5.70 and family leave $6.90. The employer line for disability is $15.00. Net pay is $2,303.72.

What each is

LineMeaning
NJ_SITState income tax from the New Jersey withholding tables.
NJ_UC_EEEmployee share of unemployment insurance and workforce development.
NJ_DBL_EEEmployee Temporary Disability Insurance contribution.
NJ_PFML_EEEmployee Family Leave Insurance contribution.
NJ_DI_EREmployer disability insurance contribution.

Each has its own wage base

The contributions apply to wages up to yearly bases set by the state, and the bases differ between the programs. The engine applies each against year-to-date wages. Send them as ytd.stateUnemployment.NJ, ytd.stateDisabilityEmployee.NJ and ytd.statePaidLeave.NJ.

Questions

Which payroll taxes does New Jersey withhold from employees?

Income tax, an unemployment and workforce contribution, a temporary disability insurance contribution and a family leave insurance contribution.

Does the employer pay anything for New Jersey disability?

Yes. The engine returns an employer disability insurance line on top of the employee lines.
