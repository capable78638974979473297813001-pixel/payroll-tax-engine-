Highest and lowest

Questions

What is the state unemployment tax wage base?

It is the amount of each employee's yearly wages that unemployment tax applies to. It is set by each state; in Omnia.tax's 2026 rules it runs from $7,000 in Arkansas to $78,200 in Washington.

What unemployment rate does a new employer pay?

Each state sets a new-employer rate, and where a state publishes one the 2026 figures run from 1% in Alaska to 4.1% in New York. Some states assign new-employer rates by industry instead of publishing a single figure.
