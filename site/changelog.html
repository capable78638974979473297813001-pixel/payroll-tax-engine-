Changelog

What's new

Engine and data updates, newest first.

Oct 27, 2026

Employer taxes on quarterly payroll

POST /v1/employer-taxes calculates New York MCTMT and the Nevada Modified Business Tax from a quarter's payroll. The MCTMT calculation reproduces the state's worked example. Also available as an MCP tool.

Oct 27, 2026

Net-to-gross endpoint

POST /v1/paycheck/gross-up finds the pay that leaves an employee a given take-home amount. It runs the engine on candidate amounts, so the answer and the paycheck are engine results. Also available as an MCP tool.

Oct 27, 2026

MCP server and paycheck comparison

Point an AI assistant at the MCP endpoint to calculate, compare, resolve addresses and check coverage with your key. POST /v1/paycheck/compare returns two paychecks and a step-by-step explanation of the difference, each step an engine result.

Oct 27, 2026

Test keys

A free test key (sk_test_) runs the same engine and data as a live key, is never billed, and is capped per minute and per day. Use it for integration work and in the site's calculator.

Oct 17, 2026

Source watch now the only scheduled automation

Rate sources for covered jurisdictions are checked on a schedule. The extra harvest workflow was removed.

Oct 16, 2026

Announcement sources added

Covered jurisdictions now track their own announcement pages.

Oct 15, 2026

Woodville, Ohio municipal income tax

1% village income tax, effective July 1, 2026.

Oct 15, 2026

Ohio rate files reconciled

Linked documents are read and checked against Ohio's rate files.
