Payroll tax glossary

The abbreviations on a pay stub and in the API, in plain words.

Payroll is full of abbreviations that mean one narrow thing. This glossary covers the ones that appear on a pay stub and in the Omnia.tax API's tax line ids, with a pointer to the page that explains each in depth.

Taxes

TermMeaning
FITFederal income tax withholding, from the employee's Form W-4. How it is calculated.
FICASocial Security (6.2% up to $184,500 in 2026) plus Medicare (1.45%). FICA.
Additional Medicare0.9% on wages above $200,000, employee only. More.
FUTAFederal unemployment tax, paid by the employer. More.
SUTA or SUIState unemployment tax, paid by the employer in most states. Rates.
SDI, TDI, DBLState disability insurance programs. States.
PFML, PFL, FAMLIState paid family and medical leave contributions. States.
EIT, LSTPennsylvania earned income tax and local services tax. More.
MCTMTNew York Metropolitan Commuter Transportation Mobility Tax, an employer tax on quarterly payroll.

Pay and forms

Questions

What does FICA stand for?

Federal Insurance Contributions Act. It covers Social Security and Medicare tax, paid half by the employee and half by the employer.

What is the difference between FUTA and SUTA?

FUTA is the federal unemployment tax and SUTA is the state unemployment tax. Both are paid by the employer in most cases.
