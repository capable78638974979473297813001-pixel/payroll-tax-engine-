Withholding and wages

Semimonthly vs biweekly pay

Same salary, different check size, almost the same year.

A salary of $78,000.00 a year pays $3,000.00 biweekly (26 checks) or $3,250.00 semimonthly (24 checks). The semimonthly check is larger. Withholding follows the check, so the two schedules withhold different amounts per check, and nearly the same over the year.

Per check

Biweekly, 26 checksSemimonthly, 24 checks
Gross per check$3,000.00$3,250.00

Net pay for the year$63,703.12$63,702.96

The difference over a year is $0.04 of federal withholding, a rounding effect of the two tables. The employee's final tax is settled on their return.

Things that differ

  • Two months a year, a biweekly employee gets three checks. Budgeting is different even though the year is the same.
  • Hourly employees may earn slightly different totals because periods end on different days.
  • Benefit deductions are often set per pay period, so 24 or 26 deductions change the per-check amount.

Questions

Is semimonthly or biweekly pay better?

Neither pays more per year on the same salary. Semimonthly gives 24 larger checks; biweekly gives 26 smaller ones, with three checks in two months.

Is withholding the same over a year?

Nearly. The two schedules use different tables, so total withholding differs slightly. The final tax is settled on the return.
