Paycheck calculation formula

One line of arithmetic hides eight rules. Here is the line and the rules.

The formula is short:

net pay = gross pay − pre-tax deductions − employee taxes − post-tax deductions

Every term hides work. This page walks the order, using the Columbus, Ohio paycheck from the engine overview.

  1. Gross pay: $3,000.00.
  2. Pre-tax deductions: $0.00 here. Each tax then decides whether they reduce its wages.
  3. Social Security 6.2% of wages up to the annual cap: $186.00.
  4. Medicare 1.45% of wages: $43.50.
  5. Federal income tax from the Form W-4: $320.38.
  6. State and local income tax: Ohio Income Tax $75.77, Ohio Municipal Income Tax $75.00.
  7. Employee taxes in total: $700.65.
  8. Post-tax deductions: $0.00.

$3,000.00 − $0.00 − $700.65 − $0.00 = $2,299.35.

What is not in the formula

Employer taxes ($333.00 here) are paid on top and never reduce net pay. And the order matters in one place: the pre-tax deductions come off before the tax, but whether they come off for that tax is a per-tax rule.

For the other direction, see net to gross.

Questions

What is the formula for net pay?

Net pay equals gross pay minus pre-tax deductions, minus the taxes withheld from the employee, minus post-tax deductions. Employer taxes are not part of it.

Why is my net pay not gross minus a flat percentage?

Each tax has its own rate, cap and taxable wages, federal income tax uses brackets, and deductions affect each tax differently. The effective rate changes with pay level and location.
