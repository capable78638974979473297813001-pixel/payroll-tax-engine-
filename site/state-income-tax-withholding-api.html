State income tax withholding API

Fifty-one jurisdictions, three kinds of method, and one request shape.

State withholding is where a payroll tax engine earns its keep: no two states agree on the form, the formula or the certificate. One request, workState: { code }, selects the right one.

Three kinds of state

MethodStates in the 2026 rulesWhat the engine does
No tax on wages9: Alaska, Florida, New Hampshire, Nevada, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington, WyomingReturns no state income tax line. Employer unemployment tax still applies.
Flat rate5: Illinois 4.95%, Indiana 2.95%, Kentucky 3.5%, Michigan 4.25%, Pennsylvania 3.07%Applies the rate to wages after the state's allowances and deductions.
Own tables or formula37 statesRuns the state's published method from pay, pay frequency and the employee's certificate.

State certificates

Many states have their own withholding form with its own questions: allowances, filing status, additional withholding, exemptions. They go in workState.certificate. Where the engine needs a fact it was not given, it refuses with a cannot_compute error that names the missing field, rather than guessing.

Employees who cross a state line

Reciprocity agreements, resident-state credit and nexus rules decide which state or states withhold. See multi-state payroll tax and the list of reciprocity agreements.

Per-state detail

Rates, wage bases and a worked paycheck for each state are on the payroll tax by state pages, and what a salary takes home in each state is under take-home pay by state.

Questions

Which states have no income tax on wages?

Alaska, Florida, New Hampshire, Nevada, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington, Wyoming do not tax wages in Omnia.tax's 2026 rules, so employers there withhold no state income tax.

How many states use a flat income tax rate?

5 of the 51 jurisdictions use a flat rate in Omnia.tax's 2026 rules. 37 use the state's own tables or formula, and 9 have no tax on wages.

What if a state needs information the request does not have?

The engine returns a cannot_compute error that names the missing certificate field. It does not guess.
