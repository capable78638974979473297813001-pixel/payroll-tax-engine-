Pennsylvania Earned Income Tax and Local Services Tax

Local tax by Political Subdivision code, not by city name.

Pennsylvania local tax is organised by PSD code, a six-digit identifier for the taxing district. The Earned Income Tax (EIT) is a percentage of wages, withheld at the higher of the resident rate and the work-location nonresident rate. The Local Services Tax (LST) is a flat annual amount. The engine reads PSD codes from the certificate.

A Philadelphia check

TaxTaxable wagesAmount
Pennsylvania Income Tax$3,000.00$92.10
Pennsylvania Unemployment Compensation (Employee)$3,000.00$2.10
PA Local Earned Income Tax$3,000.00$112.05

The EIT line is $112.05. The engine's detail: $3000.00 @ 3.74% (the resident rate is higher) — resident 3.74% (PSD 510101) vs. work-location nonresident 3.43% (PSD 510101, PHILADELPHIA CITY)

Resident in a different district

Change the home district to one with no resident EIT (PSD 880000) while the work stays in Philadelphia and the line becomes $102.75: $3000.00 @ 3.43% (the nonresident rate is higher) — resident 0.00% (PSD 880000) vs. work-location nonresident 3.43% (PSD 510101, PHILADELPHIA CITY) The rule is that the employer withholds the higher of the two.

LST

The Local Services Tax is a flat dollar amount per year, prorated over the pay periods. It has a municipal portion and a school district portion, each with its own low-income exemption threshold. Send lstPSD in the certificate. For PSD 010201 (Abbottstown Borough) the engine returns an LST line of $2.00 on the $3,000.00 check: $52.00/yr ÷ 26 periods, rounded DOWN to the cent (Act 32's own proration rule, the opposite direction from every other tax in this engine) = $2.00/period A second employer need not withhold if shown proof of LST already withheld; the certificate flag lstAlreadyWithheldElsewhere skips the line.

Why it is hard

  • 2,627 PSD entries exist in the data, and they change often.
  • The tax collector for a district (not the city) usually sets how to remit.
  • An employee can have a work PSD and a home PSD that disagree about the rate.
  • The address endpoint resolves the PSD codes so the form does not need to ask.

Questions

What is a PSD code?

A six-digit Political Subdivision code that identifies a Pennsylvania taxing district. Local tax is looked up by PSD code.

Which EIT rate does the employer withhold?

The higher of the employee's resident rate and the nonresident rate at the work location.
