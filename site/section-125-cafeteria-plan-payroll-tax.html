Withholding and wages

Section 125 cafeteria plan payroll tax

Health premiums paid before any tax is calculated.

A Section 125 cafeteria plan lets employees pay certain benefit premiums (health, dental, vision) and fund some accounts (health FSA, dependent care, an HSA) from pre-tax pay. Contributions are excluded from federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare wages. In payroll, that is a deduction whose category is section125.

A paycheck with a premium

A $300.00 health premium on the $3,000.00 biweekly check:

TaxTaxable wagesAmount
Federal Income Tax$2,700.00$254.38
Social Security$2,700.00$167.40
Medicare$2,700.00$39.15

Taxable wages for every line are $2,700.00 instead of $3,000.00. The employee pays $88.95 less tax, and the employer pays $32.85 less. Net pay is $2,239.07 against $2,450.12.

Cautions

  • Dependent care and commuter benefits have annual limits set by law. The engine treats the category as exempt; keeping contributions within the limit is for your benefits system.
  • Some states tax these differently. The engine applies each state's rule by tax.
  • Premiums for a more-than-2% S-corporation shareholder are not Section 125 benefits. Do not categorise them as such.

Questions

Are Section 125 premiums subject to Social Security tax?

No. They are excluded from federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare wages, which also lowers the employer's share.

How do I send a premium to the API?

As a deduction with category section125. The category decides which taxes it reduces.
