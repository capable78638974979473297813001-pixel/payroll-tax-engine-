Buying and building

Best payroll tax API: how to choose

The best one depends on what you are building. Here is how to decide.

Searches for the best payroll tax API assume one winner. In practice the right choice depends on whether you need only a calculation or also filing, how much local tax matters, how fast you must start, and how you will pay. This page gives criteria and an honest account of where Omnia.tax fits and where it does not.

Choose by what you need

If you needLook for
Gross-to-net inside your productA stateless calculation API with explainable lines and cents.
Filing and deposits as wellA payroll platform or a provider that files. Omnia.tax does not.
Local taxes rightA named jurisdiction list and address resolution. Omnia.tax publishes both.
To start today with no sales cycleSelf-serve keys and public pricing. Omnia.tax has both.
Forms, I-9 and a long track recordA larger vendor. See the Symmetry alternative page for where each fits.
Offline or very high volumeA library you maintain. See library vs API.

Where Omnia.tax fits

  • A small, self-serve calculation API: federal, 50 states and DC, 4,141 local jurisdictions, employer taxes, garnishment limits, minimum wage.
  • $0.08 per paycheck calculation after a 14-day trial; test keys free.
  • OpenAPI 3.1 and JSON Schema, an MCP server, and a compare and gross-up endpoint.

Where it does not

  • It does not file returns, deposit tax or pay employees.
  • It does not produce withholding forms or verify I-9s.
  • It is newer than some alternatives, which may matter to your auditors.
