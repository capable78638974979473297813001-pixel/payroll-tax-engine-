1. Who we are

OMNIA TAX LLC (“Omnia,” “we”) operates the Omnia.tax website, API, and console. For your account information, Omnia is the organization that decides why that information is used. For the paycheck inputs you submit so we can run a calculation, you decide the purpose and we process them to return the result.

The Service is for businesses that calculate payroll taxes. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

2. What we collect

Account and contact details: name, work email, business name, and the password you choose. Phone is optional; if you type one, we store it and do not text it or use it for billing. The password is stored only as a salted one-way hash. We also store the email verification code until it expires or is used, then it is not kept in the account record.

Agreement records: the terms version you accepted, the time you accepted it, the name you type when you sign the billing terms, and the IP address and user agent of that acceptance. We keep those as evidence of consent.

Billing: legal name and billing contact. Card and bank numbers are collected by our payment processor. We receive a processor reference, the brand, and the last four digits. We do not receive or store the full card or account number.

Usage and logs: timestamps, status codes, the check date, request identifiers, and approximate volume, plus rate-limit counters. We use them to run, secure, meter, and support the Service.

Messages you send us, including mail to scottholdan@gmail.com.

3. How we use it

We use information to provide and secure the Service, to authenticate accounts and API keys, to meter live calculations, to send verification codes and account notices, to detect abuse, and to comply with law. We do not sell personal information. We do not use calculation inputs to train models or for advertising.

4. Calculation inputs

Wages, deductions, W-4 fields, year-to-date figures, and jurisdictions are processed to return the calculation and the usage record. They may relate to your employees or clients. You are responsible for having the rights and notices needed to send them. Do not send government identifiers the API does not need.

5. Who we share with

Service providers that run the Service under contract, including the payment processor for billing and the email provider for verification codes. They may process information only to provide that service.

Address lookups. When you send an address to POST /v1/address, we may look it up with public geocoding services, including the U.S. Census Bureau’s geocoder, OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim, and state and county GIS services. Those services receive the address text and our server’s network address. They do not receive your account details. Addresses can be personal data about your employees; send only what a lookup needs.

We share information when the law, a subpoena, or the protection of Omnia, our customers, or the public requires it, and in connection with a merger or sale of assets, subject to this policy. We do not rent personal information to third parties.

6. Cookies

The console uses one HTTP-only session cookie so you stay signed in. It is required for the console and is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

7. Retention

We keep account and agreement records while the account is open and as long as we need them for legal, tax, and audit duties, including proof of consent and billing records. Usage logs are trimmed over time. A signup that never verifies its email address is deleted after seven days. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

8. Security

We use measures appropriate to the data, including HTTPS, hashing of API keys and passwords, and access limits. Card and bank numbers stay with the payment processor. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise that it is. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information, or to object to certain uses. Email scottholdan@gmail.com and we will respond as the law requires. If the request is about paycheck inputs that a customer submitted, contact that customer; we will help them as required. We will not treat you worse for asking.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising under the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar state laws.

Omnia is operated in the United States and stores data there. If you access the Service from somewhere else, you understand that the information is processed in the United States.

10. Contact and changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. Continued use after the change means you accept the update.
