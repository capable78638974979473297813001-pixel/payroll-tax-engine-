Local taxes

Ohio municipal income tax withholding

1,054 Ohio local entries are in the data, the most of any state.

Ohio cities tax wages, and employers withhold for the city where the employee works. That is the biggest difference from state tax, which follows residence. Ohio has many taxing municipalities, plus joint economic districts and school districts, and the rates differ. The engine resolves them by name from the certificate.

Three cities, the same pay

CityRateCity tax on $3,000.00Net pay
Columbus2.5%$75.00$2,299.35
Cincinnati1.8%$54.00$2,320.35
Youngstown2.75%$82.50$2,291.85

The engine's explanation for Columbus: $75.00 to Columbus @ 2.50% (residence and work municipality are the same) For Cincinnati: $54.00 to Cincinnati @ 1.80% (residence and work municipality are the same)

The rule that trips people

  • The work city is the one the employer withholds for, subject to the city's rules about days worked there.
  • The home city may tax too, usually with a credit for tax paid to the work city. That credit is settled on the return, so the engine withholds for the work city.
  • Some cities exempt nonresident workers below a threshold of days. The certificate carries that.

In the request

"workState": { "code": "OH",
  "certificate": { "residenceCity": "Columbus", "workCity": "Columbus" } }

You can skip the names and resolve the address with POST /v1/address. See address lookup.

Questions

Which Ohio city tax is withheld?

The tax of the city where the employee works. The home city may also tax the wages, usually with a credit for tax paid to the work city, which is settled on the return.

How do I find the right Ohio municipality?

Resolve the work address with the address endpoint, or pass the city names in the certificate.
