Payroll tax reciprocity agreements

When two states agree, an employee who works in one and lives in the other is withheld for the home state only.

A reciprocity agreement between two states means an employer in one state withholds the other state's income tax, not its own, for an employee who lives in the other, usually when the employee files a form saying so. It prevents a commuter from being withheld for two states.

Questions

What is a state tax reciprocity agreement?

An agreement between two states that an employee who lives in one and works in the other is taxed only by the home state, so the employer withholds for the home state.

Does reciprocity apply automatically?

Usually not. The employee typically has to file a certificate with the employer. The engine applies it when the request says the certificate is on file.
