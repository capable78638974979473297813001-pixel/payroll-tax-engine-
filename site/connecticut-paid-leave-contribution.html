Connecticut Paid Leave contribution

An employee-funded contribution up to the Social Security wage base.

Connecticut Paid Leave is funded by an employee contribution on wages, up to the Social Security wage base. The employer withholds and remits it; there is no employer share.

On a $3,000.00 biweekly check in Connecticut

The engine returns $15.00 on taxable wages of $3,000.00, paid by the employee. In its own words: $3000.00 @ 0.50%, capped at $184500.00/yr ($0.00 YTD already counted)

TaxTaxable wagesAmount
Connecticut Paid Leave (Employee)$3,000.00$15.00

Near the yearly limit the contribution stops. With $183,500.00 already counted this year against a $184,500.00 limit, only $1,000.00 of the same $3,000.00 check is left to charge, and the line becomes $5.00 (against $15.00 early in the year). The engine explains it: $1000.00 @ 0.50%, capped at $184500.00/yr ($183500.00 YTD already counted)

Who pays

The employee, by payroll deduction.

Points to remember

  • It is separate from Connecticut income tax (CT_SIT), which uses withholding code tables.
  • Wages above the Social Security wage base are not charged.
  • Track year-to-date in ytd.statePaidLeave.CT.
  • Employees exempt under the program's rules should be handled by the caller.

In the API

The line comes back from POST /v1/paycheck with workState.code set to CT, as CT_PFML_EE with payer: "employee". No extra field is needed to turn it on. See Connecticut payroll tax for the rest of the state.

Questions

What is the Connecticut Paid Leave contribution rate?

It is a percentage of wages, charged to the employee up to the Social Security wage base. The engine states the rate on each line.

Does the employer pay too?

No. The contribution is funded by employees.
