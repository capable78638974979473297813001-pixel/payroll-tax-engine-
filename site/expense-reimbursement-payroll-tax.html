Withholding and wages

Expense reimbursements and payroll tax

Paid with the check, but not wages.

When an employee is repaid for business expenses under an accountable plan, the repayment is not wages. It is paid with the paycheck, shown on the stub, and carries no income tax, Social Security or Medicare. In the API it is an earning with category reimbursement.

On a paycheck

A $200.00 mileage reimbursement added to the $3,000.00 check:

WithoutWith reimbursement
Gross pay shown$3,000.00$3,200.00
Federal income tax$320.38$320.38
Social Security$186.00$186.00
Net pay$2,450.12$2,650.12

Net pay rises by exactly $200.00. No tax line changed.

The accountable plan test

  • The expense has a business connection.
  • The employee substantiates it within a reasonable time.
  • The employee returns any excess advance.

Reimbursements that fail the test are wages. If you cannot tell, treat the amount as regular pay, which is the cautious direction.

What the engine trusts

The engine does not check whether your plan is accountable. It trusts the category you send, because that determination depends on facts your company holds. Send reimbursement only for amounts that qualify.

Questions

Are expense reimbursements taxable?

Not under an accountable plan. They are not wages and have no income tax, Social Security or Medicare. Reimbursements that fail the plan test are wages.

How do I show one on the API?

As an earning with category reimbursement. The engine adds it to gross pay shown but excludes it from every taxable base.
