Payroll tax calculator for bookkeepers

The numbers you need to book a paycheck, already separated.

A bookkeeper's job with a paycheck is to split it: wages expense, payroll tax expense, payroll liabilities, net pay to the bank. That needs every tax line labelled by who pays it. The engine returns exactly that, so the entries follow from the response instead of from a rate sheet.

From response to entries

For a $3,000.00 check in Texas with no deductions:

AccountAmount

DebitEmployer payroll tax expense$328.50

CreditCash, net pay$2,450.12

Debits are $3,328.50 and credits are $3,328.50. They balance because netPay = grossPay − employeeTaxTotal when there are no deductions.

Separate liability accounts for federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and unemployment tax make the quarterly returns easier to tie out.

Deductions

  • Pre-tax benefits (health premiums, HSA) reduce taxable wages and go to a liability for the plan.
  • 401(k) deferrals reduce income tax wages only and go to a plan liability.
  • Garnishments and other post-tax deductions are liabilities until paid over.

A boundary

Questions

How do I book a paycheck from the tax lines?

Debit wages expense for gross pay and employer payroll tax expense for the employer lines; credit liabilities for the employee and employer taxes and cash for net pay. The payer field on each line says which side owes it.

Do employer taxes reduce net pay?

No. They are an additional cost to the employer and are not taken from the employee.
