Questions

How much does the payroll tax API cost?

$0.08 per paycheck calculation after a 14-day free trial. There are no seat fees, no minimum and no tiers.

Are failed requests billed?

No. A request that is refused for validation or fails is not billed, and neither are address lookups or employer-tax estimates.
