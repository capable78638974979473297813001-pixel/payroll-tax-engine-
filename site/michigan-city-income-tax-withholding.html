Michigan city income tax withholding

Twenty-four cities, each with a resident and a nonresident rate.

Michigan allows 24 cities to tax income under the Uniform City Income Tax Ordinance. A resident of a taxing city owes the city's resident rate on all earnings, wherever earned. A nonresident who works in a taxing city owes the lower nonresident rate on wages earned there.

Residents who work elsewhere

When a resident of one taxing city works in another, both cities can claim tax. The engine withholds both lines and does not net a credit at withholding time, because the inter-city credit is claimed on the return. If your policy is to withhold only one, send only that city.

In the request

"certificate": { "residenceCity": "Detroit", "workCity": "Detroit" }
