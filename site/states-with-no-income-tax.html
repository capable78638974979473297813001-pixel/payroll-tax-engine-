States with no income tax on wages

No state income tax does not mean no payroll tax.

In Omnia.tax's 2026 rules, these do not tax wages: Alaska, Florida, New Hampshire, Nevada, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington, Wyoming. An employer there withholds no state income tax.

What still applies

  • Federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare, withheld as everywhere.
  • Federal and state unemployment tax, paid by the employer.
  • In some of these states, employee or employer contributions for paid leave, disability or similar programs. Washington's paid family and medical leave is an example.
  • Local taxes where a locality has one.

Questions

Which states have no state income tax?

Alaska, Florida, New Hampshire, Nevada, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington, Wyoming do not tax wages in Omnia.tax's 2026 rules.

Do employers in no-income-tax states still pay payroll tax?

Yes. Federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare are withheld everywhere, and employers pay federal and state unemployment tax. Some of these states also have paid-leave contributions.
