Questions

What counts as a calculation?

One successful paycheck calculation. Each successful calculation is one charge. A calculation that does not succeed is not charged. Minimum wage and garnishment checks on that same paycheck are included. An address lookup is not a paycheck charge.

Is there a contract?

No. Usage is billed monthly, pay as you go. Stop using your key and billing for new paycheck calculations stops.

Why do you need a card for the trial?

The trial is 14 days and a card is required at signup. Paycheck calculations during the trial are recorded and are not charged. After that, each paycheck calculation is $0.08.

What happens to an older key?

Sign in to the console and issue a new API key. The new key returns real calculations.
