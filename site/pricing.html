Questions

What counts as a calculation?

One successful POST /v1/paycheck call. Minimum wage and garnishment checks are included in that same calculation. Address lookups that don't match aren't billed.

Is there a contract?

No. Usage is billed monthly. Stop using your key and billing stops.

Why do you need a card for the trial?

Live keys bill per call once the trial ends. A card or bank account on file lets your integration keep running without a gap.

Is the sandbox free?

Sandbox calls run the same engine and are not billed.
