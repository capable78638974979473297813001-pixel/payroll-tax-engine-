Buying and building

Payroll tax engine evaluation checklist

Fifteen things to test before you commit, with the answer for Omnia.tax where it is verifiable.

The sales page of every payroll tax engine says it is accurate. A trial lets you check. This list is what to run, and it applies to any vendor. For each item the right-hand column says what Omnia.tax does, so you can hold it to the same list.

The list

CheckOmnia.tax
1. Is local coverage listed by name?Yes, 4,141 jurisdictions in 15 states on the coverage page, with partial states labelled.
2. Does it say when a rate is weakly sourced?Yes, as notices on the response. How.
3. Does it find local jurisdictions from an address?Yes, POST /v1/address.
4. Are rules effective-dated?Yes, the check date picks the year. How.
5. What happens to a year with no rules?A 422 unsupported_tax_year error, not a guess.
6. Is each line explainable?Yes, a detail sentence per line.
7. Are caps handled by year-to-date?Yes, via ytd. How.
8. Is money integer?Yes, cents. Why.
9. Are errors machine-readable?Yes, stable codes. List.
10. Can you try it free?Yes, test keys. How.
11. Is the price public?$0.08 per paycheck calculation, after a 14-day trial.
12. Is there a machine-readable spec?OpenAPI 3.1 and JSON Schema.
13. What is explicitly out of scope?Filing, deposits, payments, forms. More.
14. Does a two-state worker work?Yes, work and residence states. Example.
15. Can it explain a difference between two checks?Yes, the compare endpoint.

What to run in a trial

  • Twenty real paychecks from your current system, across your states.
  • A check at the Social Security cap, one with a bonus, one with a 401(k) deferral and one with a garnishment.
  • An employee who lives in one state and works in another.
  • A city you know is hard.

Keep the requests as fixtures. See testing fixtures.

What to ignore

A count of jurisdictions without a list, "accuracy" figures without a method, and claims you cannot rerun. A vendor's statement of its own size is useful context, not a test.

Questions

How do I evaluate a payroll tax engine?

Run real paychecks through a trial, including edge cases such as a Social Security cap, a bonus, a deferral, a local city and a two-state worker, and compare line by line against a known-good result.

What should I ask a vendor about local tax?

For the list of jurisdictions by name, how addresses are resolved to a jurisdiction, and how a rate that rests on a weak source is flagged.
