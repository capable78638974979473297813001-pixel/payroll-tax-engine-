Washington Cares Fund premium

A long-term care premium with no wage cap.

The Washington Cares Fund is a state long-term care insurance program funded by a premium on employee wages. Unlike most payroll premiums it has no wage cap: it applies to all wages, and the employee bears the whole premium.

On a $3,000.00 biweekly check in Washington

The engine returns $17.40 on taxable wages of $3,000.00, paid by the employee. In its own words: $3000.00 @ 0.58%, no wage cap

TaxTaxable wagesAmount
Washington Long-Term Care (Employee)$3,000.00$17.40

Who pays

The employee, by payroll deduction. There is no employer share.

Points to remember

  • No wage cap, so the line never stops during the year.
  • It is separate from PFML (WA_PFML_EE).
  • Washington has no income tax on wages.

In the API

The line comes back from POST /v1/paycheck with workState.code set to WA, as WA_LTC_EE with payer: "employee". No extra field is needed to turn it on. See Washington payroll tax for the rest of the state.

No. It applies to all wages.

The employee only. There is no employer share.
