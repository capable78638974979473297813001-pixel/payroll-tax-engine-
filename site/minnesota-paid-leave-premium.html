Minnesota Paid Leave premium

A shared premium that starts in 2026.

Minnesota Paid Leave is funded by a premium on wages up to the Social Security wage base. The total premium is split between employer and employee, and the employer may withhold the employee share from pay.

On a $3,000.00 biweekly check in Minnesota

The engine returns $13.20 on taxable wages of $3,000.00, paid by the employee. In its own words: $3000.00 @ 0.44%, capped at $185000.00/yr ($0.00 YTD already counted)

TaxTaxable wagesAmount
Minnesota Paid Leave (Employee)$3,000.00$13.20

Near the yearly limit the contribution stops. With $184,000.00 already counted this year against a $185,000.00 limit, only $1,000.00 of the same $3,000.00 check is left to charge, and the line becomes $4.40 (against $13.20 early in the year). The engine explains it: $1000.00 @ 0.44%, capped at $185000.00/yr ($184000.00 YTD already counted)

Who pays

Shared. In the engine's example the employee and employer lines are equal shares of the total premium.

Points to remember

  • The employee rate is the same for every employer, size included. A small-employer discount comes entirely off the employer's own share.
  • The wage cap follows the Social Security wage base.
  • Track year-to-date in ytd.statePaidLeave.MN.
  • Minnesota income tax is a separate line (MN_SIT).

In the API

The line comes back from POST /v1/paycheck with workState.code set to MN, as MN_PFML_EE with payer: "employee". No extra field is needed to turn it on. See Minnesota payroll tax for the rest of the state.

Questions

Who pays the Minnesota Paid Leave premium?

Employer and employee share the premium. The employer withholds the employee share from pay.

Is there a wage cap?

Yes, at the Social Security wage base.
