    Q1

    How much does it cost?

    $0.09 per paycheck calculated. No contract, no minimums, no setup fee, and no per-state or per-jurisdiction tiers.

    Q2

    What does it cover?

    Federal, all 50 states and DC, and local taxes down to the city, county, school district, and transit district, including Ohio, Pennsylvania, Michigan, Indiana, Kentucky, Alabama, New York City, Yonkers, and Oregon transit and Metro.

    Q3

    What if a jurisdiction isn’t covered?

    You get an explicit error or a “not modelled” line in the response. Never a silent $0.

    Q4

    How precise is the address lookup?

    Rooftop-level wherever our blend of address sources has a point for the address. Otherwise it falls back to block-level or street-range placement. Every response reports the precision it used, so you always know. It’s included in the same $0.09 per call. See the docs.

    Q5

    Do I need to talk to sales?

    No. Read the docs and start building.
