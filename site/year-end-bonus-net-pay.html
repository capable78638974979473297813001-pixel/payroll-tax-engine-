Year-end bonus net pay

The number staff actually ask about: how much will I get?

Employees quote a bonus as a gross amount and then are surprised by the check. The gap between the two is mostly federal withholding at the flat supplemental rate, Social Security and Medicare, and state tax. Here is what a $5,000.00 bonus paid on its own leaves in three states.

Why it is withheld that way

The bonus is paid separately, so federal withholding uses the 22% flat supplemental rate. State rules differ: California withholds on a bonus at its own supplemental rate, Texas withholds no state tax, Ohio uses its own method plus city tax. See supplemental wage rates.

Setting expectations

  • Tell employees the withholding, not just the gross. The stub will show it.
  • If you promised a net figure, use the bonus gross-up.
  • A year-end bonus can reach a cap: check Social Security with year-to-date wages.

Questions

How much of a $5,000 bonus do I keep?

It depends on the state. In Texas about 70% after the 22% federal flat rate, Social Security and Medicare; less in states with income tax. The table above shows the engine's result for three places.

Why is my bonus withheld at 22%?

Federal withholding on a separately identified bonus uses the flat supplemental rate of 22%. It is an advance on your tax, settled on your return.
