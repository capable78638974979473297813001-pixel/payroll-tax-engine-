Indiana county income tax withholding

92 counties, one rate each, set mainly by where you live.

Indiana's counties tax income, and the rate that applies depends on the county where the employee lives, with rules for those who live out of state but work in Indiana. Employers withhold it as a line beside state income tax. The engine resolves it from the certificate's county.

A Marion County check

TaxTaxable wagesAmount
Indiana Income Tax$3,000.00$88.50
Marion County Income Tax$3,000.00$60.60

The county line is $60.60. The engine says: $3000.00 @ 2.0200% (Marion County)

What to supply

  • The county, in certificate.county. Indiana's rule is the county of residence on January 1, or, for someone who lives out of state on that date, the county of principal work.
  • Nothing else: the county rates are in the data.

Without a county

Run the same check with no county and the engine does not guess. It returns a county line of $0.00 that says: NOT MODELLED — no county specified on workState.certificate.county. IN taxes every address. Do not treat as zero-tax. A missing certificate is visible instead of silently zero.

Questions

Which Indiana county tax applies?

The one for the county where the employee lives, set on a fixed date each year, with separate rules for out-of-state residents who work in Indiana.

Is the county tax part of the state tax?

No. It is a separate line, IN_COUNTY, beside IN_SIT.
