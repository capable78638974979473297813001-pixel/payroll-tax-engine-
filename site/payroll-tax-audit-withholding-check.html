Payroll tax audit: check withholding independently

Do not take the pay stub on trust. Run it again.

Payroll providers make mistakes. A rate not updated in January, a local tax missed after a move, a W-4 entered wrong. They are found when an employee asks, or when a notice arrives. A cheaper way is to rerun a sample of checks through an independent engine and read the differences.

A method

  • Pick a sample: new hires, anyone who moved, anyone who changed their W-4, a few high earners and a few from each state.
  • For each, collect: check date, pay frequency, earnings and deductions with categories, W-4, state certificate, year-to-date wages, work and home address.
  • Run each through the engine (a test key costs nothing).
  • Compare line by line: federal income tax, FICA, state, local, state programs.
  • For every difference, read the detail of the engine's line and the provider's method.

What differences usually mean

DifferenceLikely cause
A local tax line is missing or extraAddress changed, or the provider uses the home or work address wrongly.
Federal income tax is off by a round amountExtra withholding or credit entered differently from the W-4.
Social Security keeps being withheld at high earningsYear-to-date wages not tracked, or the base is the wrong year.
State tax differsWrong certificate answers or an out-of-date rate.
Net differs by the pre-tax deductionA deduction categorised as post-tax, or the reverse.

A sample to try

A single employee in Columbus, Ohio on $3,000.00 biweekly: federal $320.38, Ohio $75.77, Columbus $75.00, net $2,299.35. If your provider shows something different for the same inputs, find out why.

Limits

An independent engine can disagree with your provider and still be the one that is wrong, or both can be wrong for different reasons. Treat a difference as a question to resolve against the agency's published method, not as a verdict.
