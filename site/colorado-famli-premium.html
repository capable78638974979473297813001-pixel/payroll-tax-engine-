Colorado FAMLI premium

Paid family and medical leave, funded by a shared premium.

Colorado's Family and Medical Leave Insurance (FAMLI) program is funded by a premium on wages up to the Social Security wage base. The employee share applies whatever the employer's size; the employer share applies to employers with ten or more employees, and fewer than ten owe none.

On a $3,000.00 biweekly check in Colorado

The engine returns $13.20 on taxable wages of $3,000.00, paid by the employee. In its own words: $3000.00 @ 0.44%, capped at $184500.00/yr ($0.00 YTD already counted)

Near the yearly limit the contribution stops. With $183,500.00 already counted this year against a $184,500.00 limit, only $1,000.00 of the same $3,000.00 check is left to charge, and the line becomes $4.40 (against $13.20 early in the year). The engine explains it: $1000.00 @ 0.44%, capped at $184500.00/yr ($183500.00 YTD already counted)

Who pays

Shared. The engine returns the employee line; the employer share is a separate employer line when the employer owes it.

Points to remember

  • The premium is capped by the Social Security wage base for the year.
  • Employer size affects the employer share, a determination the caller supplies with employerLiableForPaidLeaveShare.
  • Track year-to-date in ytd.statePaidLeave.CO.
  • A few Colorado cities add an occupational privilege tax, which is separate from FAMLI.

In the API

The line comes back from POST /v1/paycheck with workState.code set to CO, as CO_PFML_EE with payer: "employee". No extra field is needed to turn it on. See Colorado payroll tax for the rest of the state.

Questions

What is the Colorado FAMLI premium?

A percentage of wages up to the Social Security wage base that funds paid family and medical leave, shared between employer and employee.

Is the employer share always owed?

No. Employers with fewer than ten employees owe no employer share, though the employee share is still withheld.
