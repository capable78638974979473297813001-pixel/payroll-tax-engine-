Employer taxes

Employer payroll tax calculator

The cost of an employee is more than their pay. These are the employer-side taxes.

An employer pays taxes on payroll that are never taken from the employee. They are what makes the cost of a hire higher than the salary.

The employer lines on one paycheck

For the Columbus, Ohio paycheck (gross $3,000.00), the engine returns these employer-paid lines, $333.00 in all:

TaxTaxable wagesAmount
Social Security (Employer)$3,000.00$186.00
Medicare (Employer)$3,000.00$43.50
FUTA$3,000.00$18.00
Ohio Unemployment Insurance (Employer)$3,000.00$85.50

What they are

  • Social Security and Medicare: a match of the employee's share. See FICA.
  • FUTA: federal unemployment, 6.0% of the first $7,000 per employee with a credit that normally brings it to 0.6%.
  • SUTA: state unemployment, on a state wage base from $7,000 (Arkansas) to $78,200 (Washington), at the employer's assigned rate or the new-employer rate. See unemployment tax by state.
  • State paid-leave and disability contributions where the state puts them on the employer.

Quarterly payroll taxes that are not per paycheck

Two employer taxes are charged on a quarter's total payroll, so no single paycheck produces them: the New York MCTMT and the Nevada Modified Business Tax. POST /v1/employer-taxes calculates them. For a quarter with $450,000.00 of payroll in New York's Zone 1 and $200,000.00 in Zone 2, and $300,000.00 of Nevada general-business wages less $20,000.00 of health benefits, the engine returns NY_MCTMT $2,810.00 and NV_MBT $2,691.00, $5,501.00 in all.

These are estimates for planning and accruals; the state return is the filing.

Questions

What payroll taxes does an employer pay?

The employer match of Social Security and Medicare, federal unemployment tax (FUTA), state unemployment tax (SUTA), and in some states paid-leave or disability contributions. A few places add quarterly payroll taxes such as New York MCTMT.

How much extra does an employee cost in payroll tax?

It depends on pay and state. The employer match of Social Security and Medicare is 7.65% of wages up to the Social Security cap, plus unemployment tax on the first part of the wages.

What is the FUTA rate?

FUTA is 6.0% of the first $7,000 of each employee's wages. Employers who pay their state unemployment tax on time normally get a credit that brings the effective rate to 0.6%.
