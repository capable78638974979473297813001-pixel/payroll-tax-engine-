Local payroll tax API

Cities, counties and school districts tax wages in some states. We cover them, and find the right one from the address.

Beyond federal and state tax, some places tax wages locally: municipal income tax in Ohio, earned income tax and local services tax in Pennsylvania, school-district income tax in Ohio and Kentucky, occupational taxes in Kentucky and Alabama, city income taxes in Michigan, Missouri and Maryland, and others. The local layer is the most common reason a payroll tax result is wrong, so it is the layer we built most carefully.

What Omnia.tax covers

  • 4,141 local tax jurisdictions in 15 states, every state where we found a local wage tax. The largest: Pennsylvania 2,627, Ohio 1,054, Kentucky 256, Indiana 92.
  • Each one listed by name, with its rate and where it came from, on the coverage page. 12 of the 15 states are listed in full; the coverage page marks the few where a list has a known gap.
  • 69 local minimum wage ordinances, checked on the same call as the tax.

Geocoding picks the right jurisdiction

Local tax usually follows the work location, the home location, or both. Send a work address, and a home address if it differs, and the address endpoint resolves each to the city, county, school district and the state's own codes. Those become the workState.certificate on the paycheck request, so a paycheck is taxed for the place the address is in, not the place its ZIP code suggests. The response says how precisely each address matched.

Every line explains itself

Each local tax line carries the rate, the wages it applied to and where the rate came from, so a payroll specialist can audit it. When a rate rests on a weaker source than the levying body's own document, the response says so in notices.

Questions

Which states have local payroll taxes?

Omnia.tax carries local taxes in 15 states, 4,141 jurisdictions in all, each listed by name on the coverage page. Pennsylvania and Ohio have the most; Kentucky, Indiana, Michigan, Alabama, Maryland and others have them too.

How does the API know which city tax applies?

It geocodes the work address, and the home address when it differs, to a city, county and school district, and the paycheck calculation applies the local taxes for those places.

Does it include local minimum wage?

Yes. 69 city and county minimum wage ordinances are checked on the same call as the tax.
