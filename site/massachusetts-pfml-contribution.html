Massachusetts PFML contribution

Employee share of a Paid Family and Medical Leave contribution.

Massachusetts funds Paid Family and Medical Leave through a contribution on wages, up to the Social Security wage base. The employee share is 0.46% (0.28% medical plus 0.18% family) whatever the employer's size, and the employer remits it.

On a $3,000.00 biweekly check in Massachusetts

The engine returns $13.80 on taxable wages of $3,000.00, paid by the employee. In its own words: $3000.00 @ 0.46%, capped at $184500.00/yr ($0.00 YTD already counted)

Massachusetts Paid Leave (Employee)$3,000.00$13.80

Near the yearly limit the contribution stops. With $183,500.00 already counted this year against a $184,500.00 limit, only $1,000.00 of the same $3,000.00 check is left to charge, and the line becomes $4.60 (against $13.80 early in the year). The engine explains it: $1000.00 @ 0.46%, capped at $184500.00/yr ($183500.00 YTD already counted)

Who pays

Shared. The engine returns the employee's share as an employee line; the employer share appears separately where the employer is liable.

Points to remember

  • The contribution is capped by the yearly wage limit.
  • The employer share depends on having 25 or more Massachusetts employees, a determination the caller supplies with the certificate flag employerLiableForPaidLeaveShare.
  • Track year-to-date in ytd.statePaidLeave.MA.
  • It is separate from Massachusetts income tax (MA_SIT).

In the API

The line comes back from POST /v1/paycheck with workState.code set to MA, as MA_PFML_EE with payer: "employee". No extra field is needed to turn it on. See Massachusetts payroll tax for the rest of the state.

Questions

Who pays Massachusetts PFML?

The contribution is shared between employer and employee. The employer remits it and may deduct the employee's share from wages.

Is there a wage cap?

Yes. The contribution applies up to the Social Security wage base for the year.
