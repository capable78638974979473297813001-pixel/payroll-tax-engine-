Payroll tax by state

Kentucky payroll tax calculation

What an employer withholds and pays in Kentucky, from the same rule files the API uses.

Kentucky taxes wages at a flat 3.5%; withholding is a flat-rate calculation on the wages left after the state's allowances and deductions. Employers also withhold federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and pay federal and state unemployment tax.

Questions

Does Kentucky have a state income tax?

Yes. Kentucky taxes wages at a flat 3.5% under Omnia.tax's 2026 rules, applied after the state's allowances and deductions.

What is Kentucky's unemployment tax wage base?

Kentucky's state unemployment wage base is $12,000 for 2026 in Omnia.tax's rule files. The federal FUTA wage base is $7,000 in every state.

What unemployment rate does a new employer pay in Kentucky?

Omnia.tax uses 2.7% for a new employer in Kentucky unless you send your own assigned rate in employer.stateUnemploymentRate. An established employer's rate is assigned by the state from its experience, so check your rate notice.
