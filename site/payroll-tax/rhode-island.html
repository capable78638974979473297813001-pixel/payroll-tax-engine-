Payroll tax by state

Rhode Island payroll tax calculation

What an employer withholds and pays in Rhode Island, from the same rule files the API uses.

Rhode Island withholds income tax from the state's own published tables or formula, not a single flat rate, so the amount depends on the employee's pay, pay frequency and withholding certificate. Employers also withhold federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and pay federal and state unemployment tax.

Questions

Does Rhode Island have a state income tax?

Yes. Rhode Island withholds income tax from its own published tables or formula. The result depends on pay, pay frequency and the employee's withholding certificate.

What is Rhode Island's unemployment tax wage base?

Rhode Island's state unemployment wage base is $30,800 for 2026 in Omnia.tax's rule files. The federal FUTA wage base is $7,000 in every state.

What unemployment rate does a new employer pay in Rhode Island?

Omnia.tax uses 1.21% for a new employer in Rhode Island unless you send your own assigned rate in employer.stateUnemploymentRate. An established employer's rate is assigned by the state from its experience, so check your rate notice.
