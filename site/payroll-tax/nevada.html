Payroll tax by state

Nevada payroll tax calculation

What an employer withholds and pays in Nevada, from the same rule files the API uses.

Nevada does not tax wages, so there is no state income tax to withhold. Employers also withhold federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and pay federal and state unemployment tax.

Nevada payroll tax at a glance

State income taxNone
Unemployment (SUTA) wage base$43,700 for 2026 and $45,400 for 2027
New-employer unemployment rate2.95%
State minimum wage$12.00
Local income or wage taxes in Omnia.tax dataNone recorded
Reciprocity with other statesNone

Omnia.tax rule files for 2026, with the 2027 figures that are already published. 2027 rules are provisional until the state publishes its own tables. Confirm against Nevada's revenue and workforce agencies before you rely on a figure.

Questions

Does Nevada have a state income tax?

No. Nevada does not tax wages, so an employer withholds no state income tax. Federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare still apply.

What is Nevada's unemployment tax wage base?

Nevada's state unemployment wage base is $43,700 for 2026 and $45,400 for 2027 in Omnia.tax's rule files. The federal FUTA wage base is $7,000 in every state.

What unemployment rate does a new employer pay in Nevada?

Omnia.tax uses 2.95% for a new employer in Nevada unless you send your own assigned rate in employer.stateUnemploymentRate. An established employer's rate is assigned by the state from its experience, so check your rate notice.
