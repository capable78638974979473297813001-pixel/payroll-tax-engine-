One call from gross pay to net pay.

Send a paycheck and get each tax line back with the wages it applied to, who pays it, and how it was worked out.

Request in, taxes out.

POST /v1/paycheck takes earnings, deductions, the federal W-4 and the work state certificate. Amounts go in and come out in cents.

Address to jurisdiction.

Send a work and home address once. Get back the state and local certificate to reuse on every pay run. Address lookups are not charged. Only the paycheck calculation is.

Minimum wage check.

Add an hourly rate to the same call. The engine compares federal, state and local floors and tells you which one binds.

Wage garnishments.

Pass the orders on file. The engine applies the federal and state limits to disposable earnings and returns what to withhold.
