What a payroll tax engine does not do

Scope stated plainly, so nobody assumes more than there is.

A payroll tax engine calculates. People who search for one are sometimes expecting a service that also files and pays, and the mismatch costs them time. Here is the boundary, stated for Omnia.tax and true of most engines.

What it does

  • Calculates federal, state and local withholding and employer taxes for one paycheck.
  • Returns each line with who pays it and how it was reached.
  • Resolves addresses to jurisdictions, compares paychecks and solves net-to-gross.

What it does not do

TaskWho does it
File returns (Form 941, state returns)You, or a payroll provider.
Deposit withheld taxYou, or a payroll provider.
Pay employeesYour bank or a payments provider.
Issue Form W-2 and other year-end formsYou, or a payroll provider.
Register your business with agenciesYou.
Decide whether a worker is an employee or a contractorYou, with advice.
Decide whether a payment is taxableYou, with advice. The engine applies the category you send.
Give tax adviceA qualified professional.

Why that is the right boundary

Calculation is deterministic: the same inputs give the same answer, and can be tested. Filing and deposits involve an agency relationship, authority to act and money movement, which are different businesses with different risks. A calculation API that quietly took on those would be harder to trust and harder to leave.

If you need the rest

Pair the engine with a payroll platform or provider for filing and payments, or build those yourself. See embedding a payroll tax engine and alternatives.

Questions

Does Omnia.tax file payroll taxes?

No. It calculates withholding and employer taxes. Filing returns and depositing tax stay with you or your payroll provider.

Does it give tax advice?

No. It applies published rules to the inputs you provide. See the disclaimer.
