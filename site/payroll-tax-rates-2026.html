Questions

What are the payroll tax rates for 2026?

Social Security is 6.2% each for employee and employer on wages up to $184,500; Medicare is 1.45% each with no cap, plus 0.9% Additional Medicare on the employee's wages above $200,000. FUTA is 6.0% of the first $7,000, normally 0.6% after the credit.

Which states have a flat income tax?

Illinois (4.95%), Indiana (2.95%), Kentucky (3.5%), Michigan (4.25%), Pennsylvania (3.07%) in Omnia.tax's 2026 rules.
