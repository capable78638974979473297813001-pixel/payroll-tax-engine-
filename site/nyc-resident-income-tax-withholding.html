New York City resident income tax withholding

A separate city line for residents of the five boroughs.

Residents of New York City pay a city income tax on top of New York State income tax. Employers withhold it as its own line, based on where the employee lives, not where they work. The engine switches it on with a single certificate flag, nycResident.

In the request

"workState": { "code": "NY", "certificate": { "nycResident": true } }

Points to remember

  • It follows residence. A New York State resident who lives in Westchester and works in Manhattan owes no city tax.
  • Yonkers has its own resident tax: see Yonkers income tax withholding.
  • Employers in the New York City area also owe the quarterly MCTMT, calculated by /v1/employer-taxes.
  • Paid Family Leave and DBL lines appear as well: see New York PFL.

Questions

Who pays New York City income tax?

Residents of the five boroughs. It is withheld by the employer as a separate line from New York State income tax, based on where the employee lives.

Does working in New York City make me pay city tax?

Not by itself. The city resident tax follows residence, so a commuter who lives outside the city does not have it withheld.
