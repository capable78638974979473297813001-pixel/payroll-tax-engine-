Local taxes

Alabama occupational tax withholding

Cities and counties tax wages by where the work is done.

Alabama cities and some counties levy occupational taxes on wages earned within their limits, and employers withhold them. They follow the work location, not residence. The engine carries Alabama municipalities with their source quality stated on each: a rate confirmed from the town's own ordinance is different from one that only appears in the Alabama League of Municipalities survey.

Source quality is stated

TierMeaning

Conflicting sourcesSources disagree. The response carries a notice naming the conflict.
Not modelledThe tax is known but its mechanics are not in the engine; the response says so.

Partial coverage

The data carries 30 Alabama jurisdictions, and the coverage page lists them and states that Alabama's local layer is partial. If a town is not in the list, the engine does not invent a rate for it.

Are all Alabama towns covered?

No. Alabama is partial. The coverage page lists the jurisdictions in the data, and lines that rest on a weaker source carry a notice.
