The minimum-wage check

Add minimumWageCheck to a paycheck request and the response says whether the pay for the hours worked meets the minimum for that location and date. It is answered in the same call and billed as one call.

Questions

Does every state have its own minimum wage?

No. Some states have none and follow the federal minimum. The table shows the figure Omnia.tax applies for each state.

Can a city have a higher minimum wage than its state?

Yes. Omnia.tax carries 69 local minimum wage ordinances, and the minimum-wage check uses the one for the work location.
