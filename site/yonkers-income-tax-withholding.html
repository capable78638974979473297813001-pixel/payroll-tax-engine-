Yonkers income tax withholding

A resident surcharge calculated from the state tax.

Yonkers, New York taxes its residents with a city income tax that is calculated from the New York State tax, not from wages directly. Employers withhold it as its own line. The engine switches it on with the certificate flag yonkersResident.

On a paycheck

On a $3,000.00 biweekly check the Yonkers line is $23.50 on top of state tax of $140.28. Net pay is $2,272.18, against $2,295.68 for an employee who does not live in Yonkers. The engine's detail: 16.75% of $140.28 NYS-style base tax ($3000.00 gross less $284.60 allowance = $2715.40 net @ 5.40% over $535.00, base $22.54) = $23.50

In the request

"workState": { "code": "NY", "certificate": { "yonkersResident": true } }

Points to remember

  • It follows residence, like the New York City tax.
  • A Yonkers resident has no New York City tax; the two flags describe different places.
  • There is a separate Yonkers nonresident earnings tax on people who work in the city but live elsewhere. Check the coverage page for what the engine carries.
  • Extra withholding for Yonkers has its own certificate field.

Questions

How is Yonkers income tax calculated?

As a percentage of the New York State tax for residents, withheld by the employer as its own line.

Does it apply if I work in Yonkers but live elsewhere?

The resident tax does not. Yonkers has a separate nonresident earnings tax, which depends on the rules in force.
