Withholding and wages

Bonus over $1 million: mandatory 37% withholding

Past $1,000,000 of supplemental wages in a year, the flat rate is not optional.

Once an employee has been paid more than $1,000,000 of supplemental wages in a calendar year, federal withholding on the excess must be at the highest rate, 37%, whatever method the employer normally uses. The engine tracks this from one number you send: ytd.supplemental.

A check that crosses the line

An executive has been paid $800,000.00 of supplemental wages so far this year and receives a $400,000.00 bonus. Only the first $200,000.00 of the bonus fits under $1,000,000.

TaxTaxable wagesAmount
Federal Income Tax (Supplemental)$400,000.00$118,000.00
Social Security$0.00$0.00
Medicare$400,000.00$5,800.00
Additional Medicare$400,000.00$3,600.00

Federal withholding on the bonus is $118,000.00. The line's own explanation: $200000.00 @ 22% + $200000.00 @ 37% (cumulative supplemental over $1000000.00) Social Security is $0.00 because the year's wages already passed the wage base, and Additional Medicare of $3,600.00 applies to the whole check because the employee is already over $200,000 for the year.

What you send

"earnings": [{ "code": "BON", "category": "supplemental", "amount": 40000000 }],
"ytd": { "socialSecurity": 18450000, "medicare": 80000000, "futa": 0, "supplemental": 80000000 }

What to remember

  • The threshold is cumulative supplemental wages to one employee in the calendar year, not per check.
  • Under $1,000,000 the engine uses the flat rate for supplemental wages, or aggregation if you categorise the pay as regular.
  • This is withholding. The 37% rate does not change the employee's tax liability.
  • State supplemental rules are separate and have no such threshold in most states.

Questions

What is the withholding rate on a bonus over $1 million?

Federal income tax withholding on supplemental wages above $1,000,000 in a calendar year is mandatory at 37%.

How does the API know the total so far?

You send the supplemental wages already paid this year in ytd.supplemental. The engine splits a check that crosses the line.
