Kentucky occupational license tax withholding

Local levies on wages by city, county and school district.

Kentucky's cities, counties and some school districts levy occupational license taxes on wages, and employers withhold them. They are commonly a percentage of gross wages. The engine carries 256 Kentucky entries and picks the right ones from the certificate.

A Louisville check

TaxTaxable wagesAmount
Kentucky Income Tax$3,000.00$100.48
Kentucky Local Occupational Tax$3,000.00$66.00

The local line is $66.00. The engine says: $66.00 to Louisville/Jefferson County Metro @ resident 2.20%

Why Kentucky is its own case

  • City and county can both levy, and some tax the same wages.
  • Louisville and Jefferson County are a consolidated metro government with one combined rate.
  • School district occupational taxes exist in some counties and are separate.
  • Residents and nonresidents may pay different rates.

Questions

Who pays Kentucky occupational license tax?

Employees working in a taxing city or county, by payroll withholding at the local rate.

Is it the same as state income tax?

No. It is a separate local levy on wages with its own rate and administration.
