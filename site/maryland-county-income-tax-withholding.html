Local taxes

Maryland county income tax withholding

A local tax that travels inside the state line.

Maryland collects county income tax through the state, and employers withhold it together with state tax. There are 24 jurisdictions (23 counties and Baltimore City), almost all at a flat percentage of the same taxable income the state uses. Because the county rate is part of one line, MD_SIT, it is easy to miss.

A Montgomery County check

On $3,000.00 biweekly the combined Maryland line is $228.10. The engine says: $78000.00/yr less $3400.00 std. deduction less $0.00 exemptions (0) = $74600.00 taxable; state $3543.50 + local $2387.20 (Montgomery @ 3.20% flat) = $5930.70/yr ÷ 26

Two counties are tiered

Anne Arundel and Frederick counties use their own internal brackets rather than a flat percentage. The rule file records both shapes.

Questions

Is Maryland county tax a separate line?

In the engine it is part of the Maryland income tax line, MD_SIT, built from the state rate and the county rate together. The detail text shows both.

What if I do not give a county?

The engine withholds at the maximum local rate and states that in the line's detail, so a missing certificate is visible.
